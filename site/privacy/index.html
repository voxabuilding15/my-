4. AI processing

When you use an AI feature, the relevant content (for example, the pages of a document and your question) is sent to our AI providers to generate the result, which is then stored in your account. For large documents, we create numerical representations ("embeddings") of passages so the AI can find the relevant parts; these are deleted with the document.

We use our AI providers' business APIs, under terms that do not permit them to use your content to train their models.{{aiTermsNote}}

AI output can be inaccurate. Please check important information against reliable sources.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your profile, documents, files, chats, notes, quizzes, flashcards, bookmarks, activity and usage records are erased. We keep only anonymous aggregated statistics that cannot identify you. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

10. Children

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. On your device, your sign-in session is encrypted with a key held in the device's secure hardware keystore. Access to your data is restricted to your account by database-level access rules. Codes are single-use, expire after 10 minutes and lock after 5 wrong attempts. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

Studexa's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google name and email only to create and identify your Studexa account.
