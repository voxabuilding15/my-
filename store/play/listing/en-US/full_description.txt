Studexa turns your own study material into summaries, quizzes and flashcards, and answers your questions about it — always pointing to the page the answer comes from.

UPLOAD ANYTHING YOU STUDY
• PDFs, Word documents and text files, including long textbooks
• Photograph a page with your camera; text is recognised on your device
• Organise your library with favourites and filters, and continue reading where you stopped

AI THAT STAYS WITH YOUR DOCUMENT
• Summaries of a whole document or a single page
• "Explain" and "Explain like I'm 10" for difficult passages
• Structured notes, mind maps and study plans
• Chat with your document: answers cite the pages they come from, and Studexa tells you when your document doesn't cover a question instead of guessing
• Answers in English, French or Arabic, whatever the language of the document
• Translate a page or any text

REMEMBER MORE
• Flashcards generated from your documents, reviewed with spaced repetition so you see each card just before you would forget it
• Quizzes with multiple-choice, true/false and short-answer questions, with an explanation and the source page for every answer
• Daily goal, study streak and weekly progress
• Optional study reminders

BUILT FOR STUDENTS
• Works offline for material you studied recently
• Light and dark themes, tablet layout, right-to-left support for Arabic, screen reader labels
• Free plan with daily AI limits; Premium raises the limits and unlocks larger documents

YOUR DATA
• No ads, and your data is never sold
• Your documents are used only to produce your results; our AI providers may not train on them
• Delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time

Studexa is for learners aged 13 and over. AI can make mistakes: check important information against your course material.
